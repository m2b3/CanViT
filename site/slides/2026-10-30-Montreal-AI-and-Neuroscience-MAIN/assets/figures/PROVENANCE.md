# Figure provenance

Figures reproduced from the cited papers for the 2026-10-30 talk (fair use; each is cited on its slide).
Extracted 2026-10-01. The figures the slides show were then downscaled to at most 1600 px on their longer side and recompressed (grayscale or a 256-color palette; `dinov3-features` to JPEG), so the pixel sizes in the rows are those of the extraction. Figures are committed only once a slide uses them; rows for the others record files kept locally for slides to come. Unless a row says otherwise, each file is a rectangular region of the stated PDF page rendered with poppler `pdftoppm` at the stated resolution, with no pixel edits. "PDF p." is the page index in the PDF file; "p." is the printed page number. "Native" is the resolution of the raster embedded in the PDF; a render above it is interpolated.
