CanViT-B in your browser

Click anywhere on the scene: CanViT-B takes one 128 × 128 px glimpse there and writes it into its canvas. Scroll or drag over the scene to set the glimpse's size, from the whole scene down to 5% of its side. After every glimpse, a linear probe decodes ADE20K classes for the whole scene from the canvas, including the parts not yet seen.

Everything runs in this browser: nothing below was recorded in advance.

The released CanViT-B (pretrained on ImageNet-21k) and its ADE20K linear probe on a 64 × 64 canvas, exported apart to ONNX in float32 and run by ONNX Runtime Web, the probe after each glimpse: on the GPU through WebGPU where the browser supports it, on the CPU through WebAssembly otherwise. EG-C2F chooses glimpses from the model's own uncertainty; it takes the full scene, then 4 quadrants, then 16 tiles. Default scene from the ADE20K validation set.
