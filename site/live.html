Try it in your browser

Click the scene: CanViT‑B takes a glimpse there and updates its canvas, decoded into a segmentation of the whole scene. It runs in your browser.

Code and model weights are released under the MIT license; CanViT is pretrained by distillation from DINOv3, released under the DINOv3 License. The scene is from the ADE20K validation set.
